    Money is going to four corridorsNorth to Gandhinagar and GIFT City, west to Sanand, south-west to Dholera, and north-west towards Mehsana. The map is organised around them.
    Farmland has a buyer restrictionGujarat law lets only registered farmers buy agricultural land. Check the buyer's status before looking at any farmland. Details are in the rules section.

    Rules and words to know

    Farmland: who is allowed to buySection 63 of the Gujarat Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act, 1948 bars the sale of agricultural land to anyone who is not a registered agriculturist. No confirmed relaxation was found as of 17 November 2026, although the state has been reported to be considering one. In plain words: a non-farmer cannot simply buy a farm. The usual legal routes are land already converted to non-agricultural (NA) use, or final plots inside a town planning scheme. A Gujarat revenue lawyer must confirm what applies to the buyer before any farmland is considered.

    People to line up before buying

    General guidance for any buyer.

    Revenue and property lawyer

    Runs a title search across about 30 years of records, reads the 7/12 and confirms the farmer-status rule. Should be paid by you, not introduced by the seller.

    RERA-registered broker for each corridor

    Brokers here specialise by area. Check the registration number on the GujRERA website before meeting.

    Licensed land surveyor

    Measures the plot on the ground and matches it to the official record, so you get the area you pay for.

    Chartered accountant

    Plans the tax on purchase and eventual sale, including TDS on purchases above ₹50 lakh and capital gains.
